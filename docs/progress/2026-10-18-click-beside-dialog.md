# A click beside a dialog keeps it (2026-10-18)
Found in the whole-app UI review of main. egui's `Modal::should_close()` is also true for a click on the dimmed window beside a dialog, and every dialog closed on it. In the Add dialog that threw away a paste, typed fields and a typed client secret. In the review of an Add, it discarded the plan and closed the Add dialog with it. Duplicate and Fix casing lost their name and choices. A click beside a dialog is easily a slip: the click that brings Klefki back to the front, or one that misses a button. The sign-in dialog already ignored it while signing in.
- `shell.rs`: `closed_on_purpose(&modal)` is Esc or a close the dialog asked for, not a click beside it. Add, Duplicate, Move, Fix casing and the plan review use it (Duplicate and Move added on 2026-10-19, after #26 landed them on main). Esc and Cancel close as before. The command palette still closes on a click beside it, as palettes do. The sign-in dialog keeps its own rule.
- Why this default: HTML's modal `<dialog>` closes on Esc but not on a click outside unless the page opts in (`closedby="closerequest"` is the default for `showModal()`, MDN, `HTMLDialogElement.closedBy`). Adrian Roselli ("Should a Dialog Close When Clicked Outside?", August 2026) says a dialog holding a task, or data that would be lost, should not light-dismiss. He also notes that a stray click or Esc is a real barrier for people with motor impairments.
